## B-345-1 — retired write-ahead cleanup erases the identity a replacement mount has sent

**Where:** `src/lib/coachSetup/packageCreateIntent.ts:361-368`, specifically unconditional `clearIntent(coachId, scope)` at `:367` after the awaited save notices retirement. [Exact helper](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-mobile/blob/97c9005e644ebc13731d1477bfecc270a10552fd/src/lib/coachSetup/packageCreateIntent.ts).

**Executed interleaving:** storage commits the first origin's write-ahead, but its completion promise is delayed; that origin unmounts; a replacement same-coach form hydrates the saved intent and sends its same key; the backend double commits one package but loses the response; only then the old save resolves. The retired origin assumes “never sent” and deletes the now-sent identity, so a subsequent hydrated retry emits a fresh UUID and the double contains two packages instead of one. This does not require out-of-order storage commits, account switching or sign-out. [Independent targeted proof](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-mobile/actions/runs/37229996097).

The test uses the actual helper/load path and a synthetic key-deduplicating backend; it proves durable identity deletion and a duplicate package, not a live Stripe/database transaction. [Executed probe](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-mobile/actions/runs/37229996097).

**Minimal fix rule:** retirement must not clear a persisted intent on the unsupported assumption that no other mount has sent it. Preserve the ambiguous saved key/body, or prove exclusive per-coach/per-scope ownership and coordinate retirement with replacement consumers before removal; an equality check on the key alone is insufficient because the replacement has legitimately sent that exact key. Keep sign-out wipe/drain rules intact.

**Verify:** the saved `audit119RetiredWriter.test.ts` cases must pass after the repair, including the lost-answer duplicate control; replay existing owner/unmount, write/read refusal, restart, cadence and diagnostic controls. The spec is retained at `ops/aud-119/AUD-SOL-W12-119/345-retired-writer.test.ts`. [Failing-before lane](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-mobile/actions/runs/37229996097).

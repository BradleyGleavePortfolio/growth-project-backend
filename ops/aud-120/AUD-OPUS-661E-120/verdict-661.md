## Findings

C-661-19 (new, copy truth, freeze follow-up). Location: `src/checkout/subscription-checkout.service.ts:904-935` → `:798` / `:806`. Scenario: a plan whose first invoice was paid goes `unpaid` (entitled false, not ended), then its original idempotency key is replayed. The replay skips `alreadyActive` (`:904`) and finds no secret now (`:914`). It retrieves the subscription and goes to `finishBound`, which mints an ephemeral key (`:798`). Then `storeCredentials` refuses and the replay answers `SUBSCRIPTION_ATTEMPT_EXPIRED` "This checkout timed out before it was paid. Nothing was charged." (`subscription-errors.ts:59`). That is false for this plan. A new key answers `SUBSCRIPTION_ALREADY_ACTIVE`. Probe R4b fails at head (runs 37353753255, 37354252278); R4a passes. Before round 9 the same replay handed back the spent first-invoice secret, so the round improves secrets. The issue is copy only, reachable only through a stale-key replay, and moves no money. Rule: in `replayAttempt`, a bound row whose status is in `LIVE_SUBSCRIPTION_STATUSES`, or that was ever granted, answers `alreadyActive` before any Stripe call.

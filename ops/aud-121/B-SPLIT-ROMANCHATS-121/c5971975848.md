**Root cause.** The refresh compared the stored refresh token and then awaited two SecureStore writes. Nothing ordered those writes against the sign-in screens or `signOut`, and a failed refresh signed out after a network await without re-checking the session. A check followed by an await is not a guard.

**Design: one fence for the session-credential pair** (new `src/services/sessionFence.ts`):
- Every `secureStorage.setItem` / `removeItem` of `supabase_token` / `supabase_refresh_token` moves a session generation synchronously at call time. It then waits until no other party holds the fence. This covers LoginScreen, CreateAccountScreen, appleAuth, googleAuth and signOut without editing those call sites.
- The refresh records its generation when it starts. It publishes tokens only through `holdSessionFence(startGeneration)`, which is synchronous, with no await between the check and taking the fence. A sign-in or sign-out that began earlier has already moved the generation, so nothing is written. One that begins during the two writes waits for them, then overwrites.
- A failed refresh first checks its generation (`handleRefreshFailure(startGeneration)`). After the receipt await, it signs out only by taking the fence for that generation, and holds it for the whole `signOut(undefined, { sessionFence })`. A sign-in that starts meanwhile lands after the sign-out finishes. Under the fence, `signOut` removes the keys with the pass and skips the push-token PATCH. That session's token was just rejected and cannot be refreshed. On ec2857ba, that PATCH's 401 awaited the very `refreshPromise` that was waiting on `signOut`, a wait that never ended.
- Each request records `_sessionGeneration` when first sent (the token read is retried if the keys changed mid-read). A 401 replay in a later session, including A -> logout -> A, is never sent.

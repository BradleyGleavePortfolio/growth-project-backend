### B-331-1: two conversations from the same local day get the same label, including in the delete confirm
- **Evidence:**
  - `chatDateLabel` (`src/screens/settings/romanChatsCopy.ts:68`) renders the local date only ("Thursday, October 1").
  - Backend sessions are per UTC day (`roman.service.ts:147` `dayKeyUtc`, `:188`). In Pacific time, a chat at 3 pm and another at 6 pm on October 1 are two sessions, because 6 pm PT is already October 2 UTC.
  - Both rows read "Thursday, October 1" (`RomanConversationsScreen.tsx:166`). The transcript title says "Conversation from Thursday, October 1" (`RomanConversationScreen.tsx:317`).
  - The permanent-delete confirm says "…your conversation with Roman from Thursday, October 1. It cannot be undone." (`RomanConversationsScreen.tsx:337`, `RomanConversationScreen.tsx:325`).
- **Impact:** this happens every evening for US users. The person cannot tell which of the two they are about to erase for good.
- **Minimal fix:**
  1. Add the local start time to the label used by the row, the transcript title and both confirm bodies, for example "Thursday, October 1 at 6:12 PM". Show it at least when another loaded chat shares the local date; always showing it is simpler.
  2. Add a test with two sessions on one local date whose labels and confirm bodies differ.

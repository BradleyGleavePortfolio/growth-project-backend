### B-659-8 — cancellation/pause does not fence the message transaction

Serialize delivery commitment against pause/cancel on the broadcast/run authority, not only on a recipient lease; acknowledged cancellation before commit must stop or roll back the copy. Add staged cancel/pause during preference lookup and during transaction entry, asserting no post-cancel message/card or notification; preserve already committed copies without resending. [Commit boundary](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/fa9a7cbd33c5f1c1d5108f3a3d57ea70f3177faf/src/broadcasts/broadcast-dispatcher.service.ts#L372-L419).

Stripe never deletes a subscription's draft ([Delete a draft invoice](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices/delete): "if an invoice is for a subscription, it must be voided"), so each draft not also on the open/uncollectible pages is finalized with `auto_advance=false` (`finalizeInvoice`, confirmed same id and `status: 'open'`, else `draft_not_fenced`) and then voided (confirmed `void`), each call on a CAS-renewed lease, before the other voids. Drafts count toward `TRIAL_CONFLICT_MAX_VOIDS`. After the voids, the draft, open and uncollectible pages are read again (any id not fenced -> `invoices_changed`; unknown -> `invoices_unknown`), then the paid page (`charged` -> `billed`, settled as `superseded` with `billing_started` and the billed alert; unknown -> `history_unknown`). Every non-ok result sends no DELETE.

## Findings
**B-353-9 (new; same root as B-352-9): every dispute surface says the bank reversed or took back a payment. That is false for an inquiry (owner decision 6), which moves no money.**
- **Where:**
  - `DunningBanner.tsx:28-31`: title "A payment was reversed", body "Your bank reversed a payment ...";
  - `DunningLockoutScreen.tsx:71`: "Your bank reversed a payment ... to Avery.";
  - `UpdateCardScreen.tsx:91`: "Your bank took back a payment ...".
- **Counterexample:**
  - The backend status for an inquiry pause is `kind: 'dispute'`, `reason: 'dispute_paused'`, no amount (#705 `dunning-v2.service.ts:1575-1594`; the inquiry pause is pinned by `test/dunning-v2-dispute-pause.spec.ts:269`).
  - The lockout then reads "Your bank reversed a payment to Avery. Your access has ended and billing is paused. ...".
  - Stripe: inquiries have no financial impact ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/withdrawing)).
  - 4 PROBEs fail: banner, lockout summary, Update card intro (locked) and (waived). Received, for example: "A payment was reversed Your bank reversed a payment to Avery. For that plan, access has ended ...".
- **Fix rule:**
  - Use the L1 wording from B-352-9: "Your bank opened a dispute or inquiry about a payment[ of $X] to <coach>."
  - The banner title must not claim a reversal. For example, use the backend's headline: "The bank opened a dispute or inquiry about a payment."
  - Nothing else changes.
- **Verify:** the `B-353-9` PROBEs in `aud121OpusL3_353.probe.test.tsx` turn green; VERIFY and CONTROL cases stay green.

**B-353-10 (new): on a dispute lockout, the one way back the screen offers is a route the backend blocks while the client is locked.**
- **Where (mobile):**
  - `DunningLockoutScreen.tsx:88-93`: the next step is "Message Avery to talk about restarting.";
  - `:247-254`: Message coach is the primary action for a dispute;
  - `UpdateCardScreen.tsx:477-488`: the same button.
- **How the app gets there:** the button opens Home > Messages. That route is reachable while locked on mobile (`DunningLockoutProvider.tsx:16-21`). The Messages screen reads and writes `GET/POST /messages`, `/messages/read` and `/messages/unread-count` (`src/services/api.ts:764-770`).
- **Backend:**
  - A dispute or inquiry pause sets `locked_out_at` and `entitlement_active: false` (#705 @ `2a03d7dd`, `dunning-v2.service.ts:1253-1254`, `:1274`).
  - The global `DunningLockoutGuard` (`app.module.ts:494`) then answers every route outside its allow-list with 403 `LOCKED_DUNNING` (`dunning-lockout.guard.ts:171-184`).
  - The allow-list (`:56-65`, `:79-82`, `:91`, `:101-105`) holds billing, checkout, payment-recovery, recover, auth, health, coach billing, `/roman` and three AI-consent operations.
  - `messages` (`client-messaging.controller.ts:41`) is not on it. The same is true on backend main `5da537d6`.
- **Counterexample:**
  - A dispute-paused client taps "Message Avery". Every Messages call returns 403, and the screen shows "Your plan is paused because of a payment problem, so this is not available right now. The payment screen shows what happened and what to do next." (`api.ts:105-106`).
  - The payment screen sends the client back to "Message Avery", so the client goes round in a loop.
  - The copy names a step the client cannot take. Email support still works.
- **Why it was missed:** the Opus 119 fix rule for B-353-7 asked for "message the coach" as the primary action without checking the backend allow-list. The builder followed that rule; the miss is the lens's.
- **Fix rule (operator decision; recommended default (a)):**
  - **(a) Backend:** the dunning guard admits the client's own coach DM while locked: `GET /messages`, `POST /messages`, `POST /messages/read`, `GET /messages/unread-count`, exact method and path pairs, like the AI-consent operations. #353 then needs no change, and this finding closes once that backend head is cited.
  - **(b) Mobile only:** while the account is locked, Email support leads. The next step says the coach decides and support can answer questions. Message coach is not offered as the way back.
- **Verify:**
  - for (a): a backend probe that a locked client's `GET`/`POST /messages` passes the guard while `/workouts` still returns 403;
  - for (b): a rendered dispute lockout whose primary action is Email support and whose copy does not offer a message.

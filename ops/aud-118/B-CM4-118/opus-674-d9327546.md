### B-674-5 — a lost-chargeback head-coach reversal that Stripe made but whose response was lost is never recorded, and no alert fires
**Where**
- `onDisputeClosed` calls `applyHeadCoachReversal` (refund-dispute-handler.service.ts:1197).
  - That call sends `reverse()` with no claim and the legacy amount-scoped key (:1124-1128).
  - A Stripe failure is caught and logged (:1129-1134), and the block then sets `ChargeDispute.ledger_reversed = true` (:1207), so no redelivery re-enters it.
  - Nothing retries a dispute: the sweep selects ChargeRefund rows only.
- `onTransferReversed` (:1435-1455) used to be the only writer that caught such a reversal (main and 9a512028 mirrored Stripe's `amount_reversed` into the transfer). This diff makes it observe-only, with a `logger.warn` and no Sentry alert.
- The runbook (docs/runbooks/refund-transfer-reversal-review.md:38-40) says a Dashboard reversal should be recorded "here". The reconcile route accepts only a ChargeRefund that is in review (:846-853, 409 `REFUND_TRANSFER_REVERSAL_NOT_IN_REVIEW`), so neither a dispute nor a hand reversal can be recorded with it.

**Counterexample** (red in [run 37178103384](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37178103384), `test/audit-opcm1-117-674.spec.ts`)
- The setup uses the real handler, ledger and orchestrator with the PR's harness: a 4,900-cent sale with a 245-cent head-coach transfer linked to its `head_coach_split` slice.
- A lost chargeback arrives. Stripe makes the 245-cent reversal, but the client sees `request_timeout`.
- The `charge.dispute.closed` event is delivered twice, since a fix may make the first delivery fail so that Stripe redelivers. Then Stripe's `transfer.reversed` arrives (245, fully reversed), and the sweep runs.
- Result: Stripe has reversed **245**. The local `ConnectTransfer.reversed_amount_cents` is **0**, and the head-coach slice `reversed_cents` is **0**.
  - The dispute is marked done, and the only trace is a warn line.
  - The coach payments read (`COACH_TRANSFER_SELECT.reversed_amount_cents`) and the head coach's Money keep showing 245 cents that Stripe took back.
- At 9a512028 and on main, the webhook mirror set the transfer to 245, so the transfer record is a regression. The slice was wrong there too.

**Fix rule:** one recorder for every reversal this service makes, and an alert for any it cannot attribute. For example:
- Give the lost-chargeback head-coach reversal a dispute-scoped idempotency key, and make a lost response recoverable with that same key. Either fail the delivery so that Stripe redelivers (the ledger postings are already idempotent per source), or add a retry like the refund sweep. Record it once, with its posting.
- When `transfer.reversed` reports a Stripe total above the recorded total and no refund of that purchase still owes a head-coach reversal, raise a Sentry alert (code, ids and both totals only).
- Make the runbook line name an action that works.

Stay inside the size limit by moving tests out of this piece if needed.

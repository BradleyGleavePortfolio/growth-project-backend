Money self-check:
- Webhook order and redelivery: the database sets a refund's first success time (`posted_at`) and a chargeback's first close time (`closed_at`) once each, and a redelivery or a late `charge.refund.updated` leaves them unchanged. Ledger postings are claimed once per refund or dispute (`ledger_reversed` compare-and-set). The head-coach reversal runs once per source under its scoped key. `transfer.reversed` only observes.
- Concurrency (two workers, lock order): slices and transfers move by compare-and-set, and the live test shows two refunds behind held locks both count. Postings are unique on (entry, source kind, source id). The sweep claims rows by compare-and-set on `transfer_reversal_last_attempt_at`. Each transaction takes its refund or dispute claim row first. The ledger transaction then touches only the destination and fee slices, in `findByPurchase` order (kind, created_at). The transfer record touches the transfer and then its head-coach slice. The two sets of rows do not overlap and each is always taken in the same order, so two workers cannot deadlock.
- Terminal states: only a `succeeded` refund or a `lost` chargeback moves money. A full refund flips the purchase to `refunded`, and a loss flips it to `chargeback_lost`, in the claiming transaction. Pending, failed and canceled refunds and won disputes post nothing. A succeeded refund that later turns `failed` is not un-reversed (outside this diff; Follow-up C-674-8).
- List pagination and completeness: `listAllTransferReversals` reads 10 pages of 100 and then fails closed with 503 `TRANSFER_REVERSALS_TOO_MANY`. The retry path treats that as pending and sends nothing. The sweep is bounded per run and, after B-674-11, fair across runs.
- Currency: every amount is an integer in minor units. Refund and dispute amounts are in the charge's presentment currency. The head-coach share is the integer pro-rata share of the transfer, so it stays in the transfer's currency. The storefront accepts USD only today, so presentment and settlement currency are the same. The destination and fee split (`Math.floor(amount × ratio)`) is unchanged main code; Sol's rule is to keep it.
- Copy truth: this round adds no user-facing text. Postings exist only after Stripe reports `succeeded` or `lost`. Sentry and log payloads carry ids and codes only.

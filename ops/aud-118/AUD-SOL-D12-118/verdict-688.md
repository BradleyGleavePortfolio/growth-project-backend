### B-688-6 — an old lost-dispute closure replay poisons an unrelated later payment cycle

The marker write at `1210–1223` ignores that applicability boundary and overrides the deliberate earlier-cycle exclusion at `1234–1254`. [Closure, obligation and marker logic](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/b17f514ccd8da195d18588ca4b7ca407a789f20e/src%2Fcheckout%2Fdunning-v2%2Fdunning-v2.service.ts)

**Executed public-entry counterexample:** seed a later locked ordinary-payment cycle and a durable lost dispute closed thirty days before it; the control reports no dispute cycle. Redeliver the same closure: its old timestamp remains unchanged, but the current cycle becomes disputed; `applyImmediateClear('retry')` then refuses to lift the lock and leaves entitlement false. Actual output is `{disputed:true,lifted:false,lock:<day10>,entitled:false}`, rather than `{disputed:false,lifted:true,lock:null,entitled:true}`. This is a deterministic service/fake-database proof, not a live Stripe/global-dedup test. [Exact-source replay and recovery probe](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37218555476)

Global dedup does short-circuit an already-committed event ID; this is not a claim that every acknowledged replay reaches the service. [D4 call-order context, not a D4 verdict](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/06307883100ec142aa2818fc30ee276cab26c1ec/src%2Fcheckout%2Fcheckout-webhook-handler.service.ts) [Global receipt/transaction order](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/b17f514ccd8da195d18588ca4b7ca407a789f20e/src/billing/billing.service.ts)

**Minimal fix rule:** under the state lock, derive marker applicability from the merged obligation and current cycle, including the preserved closure/cycle timestamps; replay of an already-final earlier-cycle closure must be effect-idempotent for the later cycle.

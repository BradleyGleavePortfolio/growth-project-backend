### B-681-2 — account-dependent key still permits two legacy rows for the same logical payee

**File:line:** `src/connect/fees/split-ledger.service.ts:61–64, 332–367, 371–383`. The lookup identifies `(purchase_id, kind, payee_user_id)`, but the durable unique key identifies `(purchase_id, kind, payee_stripe_account_id)`; the latter can change without changing the former. The same service explicitly supports refreshing a reconnected payee account, and the Connect lifecycle documents operator-assisted reconnection rather than an immutable-account invariant. [Ledger lookup/key/refresh](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/9de3135c2f128289ab302dff43a04e12f32b74d1/src/connect/fees/split-ledger.service.ts) [Connect lifecycle](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/9de3135c2f128289ab302dff43a04e12f32b74d1/src/connect/connect.service.ts)

This violates the retained per-purchase legacy identity and leaves duplicate ledger/replay state; it is not a claim that the probe moved money at Stripe.

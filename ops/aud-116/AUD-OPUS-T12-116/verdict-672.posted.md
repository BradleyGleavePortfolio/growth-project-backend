B-672-1 — src/packages/trials/trial-notice.service.ts:731-742 (`clientTimeZone`), used at :280 (in-app row), :332 (push body) and :359 (email `trial_end_date`, also the subject "Your free trial ends on {{trial_end_date}}"). The trial-end date is formatted in `NotificationPreferences.timezone` with no provenance check, and in UTC when no row exists. Main's rule for notification copy is `resolveRecipientTimeZone` (src/notifications/recipient-timezone.ts:5-19, from Opus B-647-1 / Sol B-647-2). Under that rule an unstamped row (schema default America/Los_Angeles, `timezone_updated_at` null) is not the person's zone, the fallback is the coach's `CoachProfile.timezone`, and there is "never a silent Pacific default". Mobile main (367e6c48) never calls `PUT notifications/timezone`, so for clients today every row is unstamped or absent. The notice says "Your free trial ends on <date>. Your card will be charged $49 then. Cancel anytime before." When the date is a day late, a client who cancels on the named day has already been charged.
  Counterexample (CI-lane run https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37173251463, `test/audit-opus-t12-116-672.spec.ts` on this head, 4 of 4 probes red as expected):
  (a) No preferences row, coach zone America/New_York. The trial ends 2026-10-13T01:30Z (9:30 pm Eastern, Oct 12). The in-app row reads "Your free trial ends on Oct 13. Your card will be charged $49 then. Cancel anytime before." Push and email format with the same zone (:332, :359). Main's resolver gives America/New_York, so the correct date is "Oct 12".
  (b) Unstamped default row, coach zone Pacific/Honolulu. The trial ends 2026-10-12T08:00Z (10 pm Hawaii, Oct 11). The notice reads "Your free trial ends on Oct 12. ..."; the correct date is "Oct 11".
  Minimal fix rule: format every trial-end date (in-app, push, email body and subject) in `resolveRecipientTimeZone(prisma, client_user_id)`. When that returns null, never print a date later than the true local end (print the time with its zone, or the earliest calendar date the end can fall on across plausible zones). Add tests for an unstamped row, no row, and the coach-zone fallback.

C-672-1 — trial-notice.service.ts:399 with :640. `sweep(now)` passes its start time to every `deliver()`, and `claim()` sets `lease_until = now + 2 min` from that value. A row reached more than 2 minutes into a slow sweep (a degraded provider costs up to 60 s per row) is claimed with a lease that has already expired, so a post-commit delivery or the next tick claims it in parallel. Probe (same run): claim with the sweep start, then a caller 3 minutes later claims again (`push_attempts` 2, expected 1). Minimal fix rule: take `new Date()` per claim (keep the sweep `now` for selection only), or compute `lease_until` in SQL from `now()`.
C-672-2 — trial-notice.service.ts:331 and :386-399. `deliver()` returns without touching either channel for a cancelled purchase (and defers on an unknown card), so the row stays `pending`. The sweep reads the oldest 50 pending rows (`created_at asc, take 50`), so 50 such rows hold every newer retry back until their trials end (up to 3 days). Probe (same run): 50 older notices of cancelled purchases plus one due notice; three sweeps deliver nothing (0 pushes, expected 1). Minimal fix rule: give a cancelled purchase's channels a terminal status (for example `skipped`) or exclude them in the query, and page by keyset like `reconcileDue`.

C-672-5 — trial-notice.service.ts:131-143. `upcomingChargeCents` sums `unit_amount * quantity`. Hosted package checkout enables `automatic_tax` (src/connect/stripe-connect-api.service.ts:519), and the sum also ignores discounts, so "Your card will be charged $49 then" can understate the real trial-end charge. Minimal fix rule: say "plus any tax" when tax applies, or read the amount from Stripe's upcoming invoice.

Minimal fix rule: correct the comment, or keep the base key after a timeout. Store the in-app row without the charge sentence, or refresh it at delivery.

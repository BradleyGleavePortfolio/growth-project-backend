## What a client and a coach get
- **Coach:** sets a free trial of 0 to 30 days on a recurring package. The recommended presets are 3, 7, 14 and 30 days (`TRIAL_DAY_PRESETS`). A trial is refused on a one-time package (`PACKAGE_TRIAL_REQUIRES_RECURRING`), on a one-time + recurring combo package (same code), and on a free package (`PACKAGE_TRIAL_NOT_ON_FREE`). A length outside the range is refused with `PACKAGE_TRIAL_DAYS_OUT_OF_RANGE`. Switching a trial package to one-time drops the trial in the same PATCH. Changing a trial does not trip the pricing lock and does not clear the Stripe price. Publish re-checks the rule. A DB CHECK mirrors the same rule.
- **Client:** sees `trial_offer: { trial_days, available, reason: 'offered'|'already_used'|'none' }` on each package before paying. The card is saved up front, and trialing access starts only once Stripe reports `default_payment_method` on the subscription. The client is charged automatically at the trial end. If they cancel during the trial they are never charged, and access ends at the trial end.
- **One trial per client per coach (server-side):** `PackageTrialUsage` has a unique `(client_user_id, coach_user_id)` index. `reserve()` uses `createMany skipDuplicates` plus CAS takeover of released rows and stale (30 min) reserved rows. It answers `TRIAL_ALREADY_USED` or `TRIAL_IN_PROGRESS` with a 409. `markStarted()` reports `owned` or `conflict`. If a second trial slips through anyway, the webhook gives it no access and cancels it after commit, so no charge is ever made. If a reservation never started (no card), it is released when the subscription is deleted, canceled or `incomplete_expired`. A trial that started stays used, even after a cancel.
- **Trial-ending notice:** on `customer.subscription.trial_will_end` (Stripe sends it 3 days before the trial ends), the handler writes a `PackageTrialNotice` row. It is unique per (purchase, trial end), so a redelivered event is a no-op. The in-app row is written inside the webhook tx. Push and email go out after commit, and a 10-minute sweep retries them (up to 5 attempts). The reconciler pages due trials by keyset `(trial_ends_at, id)`, so already-noticed trials never hide later ones (round 5). Round 4: each channel is claimed with an exclusive lease (token + 60 s expiry); only the token holder records the outcome, so a stale caller never overwrites `delivered`/`sent`; each send is bounded at 30 s; delivery is at-least-once (a lease that expires mid-send can repeat one push; the email key `trial-ending:<purchase>:<trialEndMs>` dedupes the first attempt, retries use `...:a<n>`). The push honours the client's global mute (`push_status='suppressed'`); the email is a billing notice and is still sent. A notice is also recorded when the trial starts inside the 3-day window and by the reconciler, so a one-day trial whose only `trial_will_end` arrived before the card was saved still gets it. The copy is exactly: "Your free trial ends on Oct 12. Your card will be charged $49 then. Cancel anytime before." After a cancel it reads: "Your free trial ends on Oct 12. Your card will not be charged, and your plan ends then." The date is in the client's time zone. The amount comes from the subscription's items. When no card will be charged (card removed and no customer default card) it reads: "Your free trial ends on Oct 12. No card is saved, so nothing will be charged and your plan ends then." The copy is recomputed at delivery. Round 5: card authority is tri-state; when the customer default card cannot be read, nothing is sent (no wrong promise) and the sweep retries; a notice is never recorded with "no card" copy on a failed read. No notice is sent when the trial never started (no access yet), when the trial is already over, or when the subscription is not trialing.
- **Read model:** `purchases[].trial = { state: none|trialing|setup_incomplete|ended|not_eligible, trial_days, ends_at, will_charge, no_charge_reason: null|'cancelled'|'no_card', charge_amount_cents, currency }` drives "Trial ends Oct 12" on Home and Your plan. Round 4: `will_charge` is true only when a card will really be charged (subscription card as last observed in `card_on_file`, or the customer default card); access is a separate fact (a started trial keeps access after the card is removed). A purchase that lost the one-trial race reads `not_eligible`.
- **Lost one-trial race (round 4):** the obligation to cancel is a `PackageTrialConflict` row written in the webhook transaction. It is settled right after commit and by a 5-minute sweep with backoff until Stripe confirms; while it is owed, no later event grants trial access. If every cancel failed until Stripe billed the regular price, the row is `superseded`, the paying client gets normal paid access, and support gets one alert to refund (`TRIAL_CONFLICT_SUPERSEDED`); 3 failed attempts alert once (`TRIAL_CONFLICT_CANCEL_FAILING`). Each alert has its own receipt (`alerted_at`, `billed_alerted_at`), so a cancel-failing alert never suppresses the billed alert (round 5). Worker logs, `last_error` and Sentry tags carry closed codes only (`trial-diagnostics.ts`; round 5). Runbook in `docs/stripe-setup.md`.

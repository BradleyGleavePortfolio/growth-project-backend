**Normal-user story:** A coach near the end of their AI allowance can keep generating programs without consuming any credits whenever a generation costs more than the remaining allowance. [Coach AI caller](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/41493e417143c74140fdd5c7cf6957b35513d1a3/src/ai/coach/coach-ai.service.ts) / [Budget debit](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/41493e417143c74140fdd5c7cf6957b35513d1a3/src/ai-credits/coach-ai-budget.service.ts)
- Deterministic ordinary sequential trace: used=3,999 cents, ceiling=4,000, generation cost=5; precheck allows `3999 < 4000`, debit predicate `3999 <= 3995` fails, used stays 3,999, and every following same-cost generation is again allowed without a debit. This is not a one-call overshoot or a simultaneous-request problem. [Coach AI caller](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/41493e417143c74140fdd5c7cf6957b35513d1a3/src/ai/coach/coach-ai.service.ts) / [Budget debit](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/41493e417143c74140fdd5c7cf6957b35513d1a3/src/ai-credits/coach-ai-budget.service.ts)

**Smallest fix:** account for the incurred spend even when it crosses the ceiling, or atomically consume remaining headroom on this insufficient-headroom outcome, so the next ordinary call gets 402 before contacting Anthropic. Add a sequential regression: 3,999/4,000 and 5-cent cost, first completed call consumes the remaining allowance, second call has no provider invocation or new draft.

### B (must fix)

**B-349-1: the charge detail presents a recurring plan's whole history as one payment.**

*What a coach sees:*
- **Breakdown contradicts the price.** A client is in month 3 of a $100/month plan, and the coach taps that charge. The page heads "$100.00 / Paid / Monthly" and then shows "How this adds up to your net: Clients paid $300.00 ... TGP fee (2%) -$6.00 ... Net to you $28x". It reads as if the client was charged $300, or TGP took 6%.
- **Failed renewal reads as no money at all.** If the latest renewal fails, the same page says "the client was not charged and nothing from this charge reaches your payouts", and it hides the breakdown. The earlier months were paid and paid out.

*Evidence:*
- `MoneyChargeScreen.tsx:120` heads the page with `money(c.amountCents)`, which is one period's price.
- `:95-103` and `:141` render backend `getChargeBreakdown`. That function folds **every** ledger slice of the ClientPurchase (`coach-money.service.ts:1548-1585`, no `stripe_charge_id` filter).
- Renewals post their own slices on the same purchase:
  - `SplitLedgerEntry` is unique on `[purchase_id, kind, payee_user_id, stripe_charge_id]`;
  - `purchase-split-handler.service.ts:77-97` settles "every paid invoice of the subscription", using `invoice_amount_cents` / `invoice_charge_id`.
- A past_due plan maps to `state: "failed"` before the paid check (`toCharge`, `coach-money.service.ts:1655` vs `:1656-1660`), so `noMoneyMovedCopy("failed")` (`MoneyChargeScreen.tsx:43-47,108,133-139`) applies to a plan that has paid before.

*Normal-user story:* a coach whose client has paid a $100 monthly plan three times taps that charge and reads that the client paid $300 for a $100 charge and that TGP took $6, or, after one failed renewal, that the client was never charged and nothing reached payouts.

*Fix rule (smallest, mobile only, no backend change):* for `billingType === "recurring"`, say what the numbers are.
1. Header: the price with its cadence ("$100.00 a month").
2. Breakdown heading "This plan so far", with the first row "Clients paid so far".
3. For a failed recurring plan with ledger money (`settled`), say the latest payment did not go through, keep the breakdown of what earlier payments paid, and keep the Needs attention pointer.
4. Leave one-time charges as they are.

*Test:* a recurring charge whose breakdown price is 3x its amount shows the plan-so-far wording, and a failed recurring charge with `settled: true` shows the breakdown and no "was not charged". The operator decision on a backend per-payment breakdown is in the report; the default is this mobile copy fix now.

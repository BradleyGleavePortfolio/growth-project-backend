## Findings
**B-353-6: a dispute shows a lock date and a grace period.**
- **Where:** `src/entitlements/dunning/DunningBanner.tsx:25-33` (rendered for any `past_due` at `:61`) and `UpdateCardScreen.tsx:85-88` (pre-lock dispute intro).
- **What the screens say:**
  - The banner: "Access pauses on Sunday, October 11 unless it is sorted out by then."
  - The pre-lock intro omits any end of access, so access reads as continuing.
- **What the ruling requires:** a dispute on a recurring plan ends access at once and pauses billing. There is no lock date and no grace period. The job entry names a lock date for a dispute as a B.
- **Counterexample:** PROBEs "the dispute banner names no lock date and no 'unless it is sorted out' condition" and "the dispute Update card intro (pre-lock) does not imply access continues" fail (run 37229975212).
- **Fix rule:**
  - A dispute cycle never renders a lock date, a deadline or keep-access wording, in any state.
  - If the server reports `kind: 'dispute'` with `state: 'past_due'` (today's #691 compressed cycle), the banner says only the ruling text (access has ended, billing is paused, the coach decides on restarting) and offers Message coach.
  - The app never decides a lockout itself; the server stays the only source of the lock.

**B-353-7: the dispute lockout, Update card, End my plan and banner copy do not say what the ruling requires.**
- **Where:**
  - `DunningLockoutScreen.tsx`: `:57-60` (summary), `:74-77` (next step), `:90-92` (end-plan body), `:193` (title "Your plan is paused"), `:208-225` (Email support primary; Update card still offered), `:273` ("Already paid? Pull down to check again.");
  - `UpdateCardScreen.tsx:88` ("email support to sort it out"; "A card saved here is used for your future payments") and `:338`;
  - `DunningBanner.tsx:31`.
- **What the screens say:**
  - Summary and next step: "Your bank reversed an earlier payment of $150.00 to Avery, so your plan is paused. ... Saving a new card does not settle it. To sort it out, email <support email> or message Avery."
  - End my plan: "Your access ends now." Access has already ended under the ruling.
- **What the ruling requires:** access has ended, billing is paused, the coach decides on restarting. Nothing restores automatically.
- **Why it fails:** support is presented as the way to "sort it out" (an implied restore). The coach-restart rule is never stated. The footnote invites a payment check that cannot restore access.
- **Counterexample:** 5 PROBEs fail (run 37229975212).
- **Fix rule:**
  - Use the shared L1 dispute sentence (B-352-7) on every dispute surface.
  - Dispute title: "Your access has ended".
  - Primary action: message the coach. Support stays secondary, for questions only.
  - Hide Update card and the "Already paid?" footnote for disputes.
  - The End my plan body for a dispute must not say "access ends now" (access has already ended). It says only what the server's cancel does for a disputed plan under D2c.
  - No "sort it out", no "settle", no future-payment line while billing is paused.
- **Verify:** this probe spec (7 PROBE) plus the builder's failing-before tests. Update the existing dispute tests at `dunningLockoutOwnership.test.tsx:390-472`, which pin the old wording.

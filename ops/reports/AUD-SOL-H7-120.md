A Connect already waiting on the consent-session read or native authority creation continues after the immediate SecureStore deletion, creates a fresh authority and commits a grant bound to it. At the durable-native-write-before-JS-completion boundary, the queued second revocation has not run; a fresh JS lifetime retaining both stores authorizes automatic refresh without a new Connect. The uninterrupted full-drain control revokes correctly; another account is unauthorized. This is an **interrupted** sign-out / permitted native scheduling proof, not completed-signOut revival, hardware reproduction, real health data or a network upload. [Two concrete interleavings](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-mobile/actions/runs/37341997614).

Minimal fix: epoch/session fencing after every awaited consent-session/authority operation and immediately before authority creation and native grant publication, or an equivalent durable protocol that makes a stale pre-sign-out writer incapable of rebinding to a post-revocation authority. Keep the second revocation as needed; it alone is not crash durability.

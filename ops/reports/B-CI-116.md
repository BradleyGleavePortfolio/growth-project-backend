## Root cause
ts-jest without isolatedModules builds a full TypeScript LanguageService in every jest worker (3 on the 4-vCPU public-repo runner) and type-checks every transformed file against the whole program, including the 24 MB generated Prisma typings. Each worker's program converges toward a full-repo type-check plus its test runtime, and crosses the 4 GB heap after ~300-400 s. The victim is whichever small, late-sorted suite the worker holds at that moment.
Local A/B, same 27 specs, same order, --runInBand, node --expose-gc (retained heap): type-checking 1396 -> 2005 (spec 4) -> 2124 MB (spec 21); transpile-only 75 -> 294 -> 386 MB. Over 64 suites transpile-only stays 270-440 MB. 10 service specs cold: 47 s vs 9 s.

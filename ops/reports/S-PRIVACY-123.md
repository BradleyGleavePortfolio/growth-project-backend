### Owner-ready replacement — community visibility

For `/privacy` “Who can see your data” and `/consumer-health-privacy` “Categories we share”, after the opt-in fix:

> Members of the community spaces you join can see your display name and the posts, comments, reactions, shared wins and group messages you choose to share in those spaces. Messages sent directly to your coach are not group messages. If you opt in to a leaderboard, other clients assigned to the same coach can see your display name, rank and participation information, such as workout counts or a habit-consistency score and its change. The habit score uses check-in, workout, meal-log and coach-message activity, not the content of your private messages. You can opt out to hide your leaderboard entry.

Basis: community content and coach/private-message categories are already disclosed, while the leaderboard code calculates the additional activity-based score and exposes rank and change.

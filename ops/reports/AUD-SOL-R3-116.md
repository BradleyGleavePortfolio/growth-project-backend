### Confirmed findings and minimal fix rules

1. **B-680-1 — subscription snapshot authority**, `checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts:1000–1039`: deletion followed by an older active update revives access/content; first paid activation followed by older incomplete creation removes access, with two failing assertions. Fix: canonical state resolved out-of-tx, terminal-state protection and a revision/final-state fence rechecked under the write lock; never put Stripe HTTP inside the transaction or use a timestamp-only total order. The pre-existing overwrite plumbing makes this piece's new native lifecycle unsafe, so this is material, not unrelated optional debt. [Changed native activation](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/2b10687c63cf02e0181339b684634ff9cfbeb803/src/checkout/checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts#L1000-L1040) [Executed proof](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37174541947)
2. **B-680-2 — first-invoice/terminal-attempt dunning**, `checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts:71–78,1450–1488`: first-invoice failure delivered after success changes active to past_due and calls dunning; an expired never-entitled attempt also enters dunning, with two failing assertions. Fix: preserve first-invoice origin independently of current row status, preserve terminal attempts, ignore settled earlier invoices, retain genuine post-trial/renewal failures, and unify the corrected helper with dunning when the second stack lands. [New failure guard](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/2b10687c63cf02e0181339b684634ff9cfbeb803/src/checkout/checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts#L1450-L1488) [Executed proof](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37174541947)
3. **B-680-3 — entitlement is not payment**, `checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts:1035–1056,1402–1406`: a carded free trial records/notifies the $49 renewal snapshot, real paid trial conversion does not invoke the first-payment service, and a $148 combo first invoice records $49, with three failing assertions through real `CoachFirstPaymentService`/`FirstPaymentEmitter`. Fix: content on first entitlement; money primitive on authoritative positive paid invoice even if already entitled, using actual paid amount/currency in the same transaction; never consume the payment key for trials/zero-due invoices or merely active subscription snapshots. [New call site](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/2b10687c63cf02e0181339b684634ff9cfbeb803/src/checkout/checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts#L1035-L1056) [Executed proof](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37174541947)
4. **B-680-4 — durable one-trial usage**, `checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts:54–63,1031,1384`: conversion-first delivery grants access without a trial-consumption marker, then cancellation permits another seven-day trial with the same coach, with one failing real-checkout/lifecycle assertion. Fix: persist consumed trial history from authoritative started/carded evidence even if the first observed subscription state is active/converted, preserve through cancellation, and refuse a second trial. This native bug is distinct from the separate trials-stack integration obligation. [Marker rule](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/2b10687c63cf02e0181339b684634ff9cfbeb803/src/checkout/checkout-webhook-handler.service.ts#L54-L64) [Executed proof](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/actions/runs/37174541947)

Stripe explicitly disclaims ordered delivery and advises retrieving missing/current objects; event timestamps are second-resolution and must not alone be treated as a total-order fence. [Stripe webhook contract](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks)

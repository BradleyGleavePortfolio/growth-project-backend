- B2 Daily check-in never saves. A client fills in Habits & Check-in and taps Save Check-in; every save fails with a 400 ("property sleep_quality should not exist, property stress should not exist") because the app sends sleep_quality and stress (src/screens/client/HabitsScreen.tsx:235-236) and the backend DTO has no such fields with forbidNonWhitelisted on (backend src/check-ins/check-ins.dto.ts, src/main.ts:98). The coach never receives any check-in.
- B3 Coach sees every client's weight trend backwards. A coach opens a client > Progress: "First" shows the newest weight, "Latest" the oldest and "Change" has the wrong sign (a client who lost 10 lb shows +10.0 in warning colour), because getClientSummary returns weight_logs newest-first and ProgressTab treats index 0 as first (src/screens/coach/client-detail/ProgressTab.tsx:24-45).

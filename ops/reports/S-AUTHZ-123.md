[Leaderboard query and response](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/community/community.service.ts#L390-L423)

[Implementation](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/community/community.service.ts#L390-L423) [Route guards](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/community/community.controller.ts#L97-L101)

[Wins access revocation](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/community/community.service.ts#L434-L450) [Durable ban contract](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/community/community-ban.ts#L4-L16)

Smallest fix: reuse the existing workspace/removal authorization before fetching teammates' session counts; return a coded refusal or an own-only result for removed/banned viewers, and restrict peer rows to authorized community members. Preserve the coach's authorized management view.

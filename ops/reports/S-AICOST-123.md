## Worst normal-use day: 100 active clients

**Planning assumptions, not production telemetry:** each client asks 50 ordinary 100-token Roman questions, each answer uses the full 1,024-token output allowance, and the system/grounding input starts at a conservative 6,000 tokens. Retained history follows the code's 30-message tail and removal of a leading assistant, giving at most 15 user messages plus 14 previous answers. These are long ordinary conversations, not adversarial inputs. ([Roman context and output limits](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/roman/roman.service.ts), [Roman limits](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/roman/roman.constants.ts))

For turn \(j\), modeled input is \(6{,}000 + 100\min(j,15) + 1{,}024\min(j-1,14)\), and provider cost is \((3I+15O)/1{,}000{,}000\) USD. This produces **$0.03366 for the first turn**, **$0.080868 for a mature long-history turn**, and **$368.934 requested provider spend** across 5,000 turns before admission; rounded pool debits would total **$410** if all requests were admitted. ([History implementation and cost formula](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/roman/roman.service.ts), [Token rates](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing))

**Actual Roman admission remains at most $100 for the day**, assuming enough funded coach pools, because the global breaker stops further paid calls; if all 100 clients share one fresh default coach pool with no packs, that **$40 monthly pool** stops coached Roman earlier, and rounded-up debits mean actual provider spend is lower still. The raw $368.934 is demand, not a bill Roman can incur through its audited admission path. ([Roman admission and debit](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/roman/roman.service.ts), [Default pool](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/ai-credits/ai-credits.constants.ts))

A third call does not fit its 6,600-token reservation after those 6,100 consumed tokens. This illustrates a material shared-pool bypass without heavy usage or unusual requests. ([Guide quota and provider call](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/blob/5230306cb63df7290459bb362340a42f385f39d5/src/ai/ai.service.ts), [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing))

You have copied the link. The next step is to use it once.

Pick a friend, a family member, or a second device of your own.
Send the link the way you would normally send it to a client — by
text, email, or a DM. Have them open the link, install the app,
sign in, and confirm they appear in your roster.

Doing this once before a real client uses the link tells you two
things: the technical flow works, and the message you wrote
alongside the link reads the way you want it to.

# Invite your first client

The [published first-client guide](https://app.trygrowthproject.com/help/first-client)
is maintained in `src/public-pages/help-pages.html.ts`.

In the coach mobile app, open **Invite your first client** on Overview,
**Clients → Invite**, or **Settings → Invite Codes**.
Send the complete link and code; check any expiry date or use limit.
Inviting and messaging do not require a coach subscription.

The browser landing shows the coach name, business name when set, code
and device-specific app instructions. After installing, the client should
open the invite again or enter the code when creating an account.
Confirm the completed join appears in **Clients**, then open the client
or **Messages** to start a conversation.

An invite does not transfer a client already linked to another coach.
Contact support about that account rather than asking the client to delete it.
